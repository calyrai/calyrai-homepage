# AFLOWTEX-Endo

Endovascular Aortic Flow and Thromboembolism Exploration

AI-assisted CFD / DPD framework for branched aortic stent-graft optimization.

## Objective

We want to turn patient-specific aortic models into an interactive research tool. Change vessel geometry, blood flow or stent-graft design and explore how the predicted flow responds.

We will use detailed simulations on VSC-5 to train fast surrogate models, then test their predictions against independent simulations and measurements. The aim: compare designs, expose uncertainty and identify what needs testing next.

## Core assets

- Aorta DB — anatomy and imaging
- CFD Hemodynamics — OpenFOAM
- DPD Thromboembolism — LAMMPS
- Clinical validation
- Surrogate foundation model

## Methodology

1. Patient-specific reconstruction — MRI / CT
2. CFD hemodynamics
3. DPD thromboembolism
4. Surrogate modelling and UQ
5. Design exploration
6. Clinical decision support

## Phases

### Foundation

DB v1.0, workflows and white paper.

### Validation

Patient cases, experiments, datasets, surrogate prototypes and ASC validation strategy.

### Translation

Clinical and industrial demonstrations, regulatory pathway and commercial pathway.

## Impact

- Less thromboembolism
- Better design
- Faster decisions
- Patient benefit

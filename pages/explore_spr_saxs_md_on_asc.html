SPR + SAXS + MD on ASC: from data to mechanism
SAXS DOI pending

Abstract

This talk is deliberately concrete. It walks through one real workflow for IgG adsorption on Protein A resin using SAXS, SPR, coarse MD, LAMMPS, and Slurm on ASC. The central point is that the mechanism is not inferred from one dataset alone: simulations generate candidate systems, SAXS and SPR provide distinct constraints, and only models that satisfy both survive. Figure treatment is intentionally abstract and schematic so the logic of the workflow stays central.

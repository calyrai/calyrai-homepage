Nexus — Data-governed scientific infrastructure for controlled inference

Nexus is the scientific data warehouse and orchestration layer of Calyr.ai. It keeps experiments, derived representations, model states and reportable outcomes inside one governed system so interpretation remains linked to the data and assumptions that produced it.

Core Role
Warehouse

Experimental records, metadata, derived features and model-side states stay linked instead of being scattered across ad hoc files.

Method
Control

Inference is staged through governed transformations, with each reduction step attached to its provenance.

Outcome
Traceability

Claims, scores and reports remain connected to the underlying data warehouse rather than detached from their origin.

Data warehouse concept

EXPERIMENT → REPRESENTATION → MODEL STATE → RESULT → REPORT

Nexus is not just a machine-learning page and not just a workflow UI.

It is the governed middle layer where raw measurements, experimental context, derived representations and model-facing states are collected into one coherent scientific object.

1 Experiments Measurements enter with metadata, run context and acquisition constraints intact.
2 Representations Signals are transformed into governed features, embeddings and coordinate systems that remain attached to the source record.
3 Model states Fits, constraints, candidate mechanisms and uncertainty estimates are stored as explicit states rather than hidden intermediate artifacts.
4 Controlled outputs Reports, scores and publication-facing results can always be traced back to the warehouse object that produced them.

$$S_i \;\longrightarrow\; C_i(\theta), \quad \theta \in \bigcap_i C_i$$

$S_i$ experimental signal  ·  $C_i$ induced constraint  ·  $\theta$ molecular state

Signals do not fit models. Signals constrain reality.

Scientific and conceptual architecture

Nexus holds two coupled voices at once: an operational scientific architecture that reviewers can recognize immediately, and a conceptual architecture that explains what kind of object the full apparatus-code-signal-control system becomes.

Operational scientific core

Scientific modules

  • Structural layer: AlphaFold, SAXS, PDD, and resin morphology define geometry, accessibility, and heterogeneity.
  • Interfacial kinetic layer: SPR and adsorption operators define multistate binding and constitutive kinetics.
  • Transport layer: packed-bed convection-diffusion executes the adsorption law under flow.
  • Geometric substrate layer: LAMMPS packings and OpenFOAM renormalize porosity, tortuosity, and accessibility.
  • Control layer: pH, temperature, ionic strength, solvent composition, and forcing functions perturb the system.
  • Emergent-output layer: chromatograms, retention, tailing, hysteresis, and resonance-catastrophe regimes become the readout.
Meta-theoretical frame

Theoretical interpreters

  • Flusser: apparatus, program, and technical image explain how observables are generated and mediated.
  • Wiener: feedback, control, and dynamic regulation explain coupled forcing and response.
  • Shannon: signal and information justify multi-channel state reconstruction.
  • Kittler: code is materially instantiated in transport equations, adsorption kinetics, and device logic.
  • Prigogine: irreversible, driven, non-equilibrium dynamics explain emergence and instability.

Experimental layers

The homepage is organised by experimental layers that all feed the same warehouse logic. Each layer keeps its own native signal, but the interpretation path stays governed and comparable.

Chromatography becomes an apparatus-mediated nonlinear transformation system: adsorption acts as constitutive code, packed-bed flow executes that code, and the measured traces become technical images of underlying state.

Nexus — The Operational Interface of Calyrai

The Nexus is a live representation of the scientific workflow, where experiments, models, and interpretations converge into a unified representation space.

What the Nexus Does

The Nexus is not a visualization of results.
It is the operational structure of the system.

Each node represents a dataset mapped through a layered inference process:

Raw measurement
-> feature representation
-> PCA projection
-> parameter inference
-> scientific insight

The Nexus functions simultaneously as:

  • a data warehouse interface
  • an analysis environment
  • a representation space
  • a hypothesis engine.

The Nexus Pipeline

Every dataset moves through the same transformation sequence:

Stage Meaning
Raw experimental measurements
Features extracted signal descriptors
Representation PCA geometry
Parameters physical interpretation
Insight mechanistic understanding

The graph displays these stages as connected layers.

How to Navigate the Nexus

  • Click raw nodes to inspect experiments
  • Explore PCA space to compare datasets
  • Follow edges to trace transformations
  • Select parameter nodes to view physical models

The drifting motion reflects relationships in representation space.

Why the Nexus Matters

The Nexus enables:

  • integration of heterogeneous experiments
  • cross-modality comparison
  • real-time interpretation
  • discovery of hidden structure in data

It transforms experimental workflows into navigable knowledge systems.

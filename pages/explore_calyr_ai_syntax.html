Abstract

This presentation formalizes the language and control-plane layer behind Calyr workflows. It introduces Calyr_ai_syntax as the symbolic expression system, Nexus as the declarative execution membrane, and the shift from merely simulatable pipelines toward constructable scientific systems. Within the current Explore structure, it sits in the SAXS branch because it underwrites the typed representation, evaluation, and execution logic that the structural pipeline depends on.

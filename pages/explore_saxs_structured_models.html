From SAXS signals to structured models
SAXS DOI pending

Abstract

This application talk shows how the SAXS program becomes computationally operational. Scattering signals are transformed into structured representations, PCA defines a navigable low-dimensional structural space, coarse models attach those modes to interpretable geometry, and ASC provides the compute layer for systematic forward exploration. The result is a closed model-data loop rather than a one-shot inversion.

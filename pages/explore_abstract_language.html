Abstract

This presentation develops the representation layer needed to make SAXS and related signals computationally interpretable. Its central claim is that data is not inherently meaningful: a signal becomes actionable only when stored in a model-native language of entities, states, transitions, and observables. Within the new Explore structure, it sits in the SAXS branch because it underwrites the structural interpretation pipeline rather than standing as a separate generic category.

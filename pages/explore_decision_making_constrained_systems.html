SAXS · Presentation 06

Decision-making in constrained molecular systems
SAXS DOI pending

Abstract

This presentation reframes model-building as constrained decision-making. SAXS, SPR, and MD do not converge automatically to a unique mechanism; instead, they define a joint constraint system over possible models. Abstraction reduces the data to a usable parameter space, constraints remove invalid explanations, and decision emerges as structured model selection. The FAIR-data angle is made explicit: FAIR means Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable, and it is essential here because only standardized, interoperable, interpretable abstractions support defensible scientific decisions.

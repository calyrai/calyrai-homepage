Explore

Calyr.ai presentation overview

Explore is organized as three coherent presentation tracks. `SPR` contains the binding and adsorption talks. `SAXS` contains the structural inference talks. `NEXUS` contains the Calyr AI syntax and data-warehouse architecture talks. Each landing page is prepared as a presentation record with an abstract, DOI status, and a related-talk bibliography.

Current series

SPR

From data to model: linking dynamics and adsorption SPR-focused pitch on why kinetics and isotherms are two limits of one process and must be modeled together. We model the process, not the curve One kinetic adsorption model generates both the time trace and the equilibrium isotherm.

SAXS

Signal-governed molecular inference Calyr.ai overview centered on constraint-based inference, typed execution, and structural reconstruction. SAXS: the model completes the measurement Why SAXS data define an equivalence class of structures and how priors regularise the inverse problem. An abstract language for data organization Why scientific signals need a shared, model-native representation before they become interpretable. From SAXS signals to structured models SAXS, PCA, coarse modeling, and ASC connected as one navigable model-space workflow. From signal to decision Why models are necessary: abstraction, constraints, Bayesian filtering, and decision under ambiguity. Decision-making in constrained molecular systems Abstraction, constraints, and FAIR linkage as a framework for selecting valid models. SPR + SAXS + MD on ASC: from data to mechanism Concrete workflow talk linking SAXS, SPR, LAMMPS, Slurm, and mechanism selection on ASC. PDB → SAXS → Folding Landscape: A Dmax-Free Framework PDB to PDD to PCA to coarse-grained ranking, with the Fold Admissibility Filter as the decision layer.

NEXUS

calyr.ai.syntax The unified AI syntax within Calyr.ai, grounded in the Nexus warehouse model from experiment to report. nexus.warehouse Nexus as the application layer for biophysics: an abstract language for data organization, entity states, and model-native storage. Calyr Architecture Clean three-layer system design: Calyr (platform), Nexus (engine), Parvotec (application). Investor-ready architecture with clear separation of responsibilities.

SAXS · Presentation 08

PDB → SAXS → Folding Landscape: A Dmax-Free Framework
SAXS DOI pending

Abstract

This presentation is a full pipeline talk rather than a conceptual sketch. It starts with PDB structures, computes pair-distance distributions directly, removes the dependence on an explicit Dmax cutoff through a frac-fit representation, projects the resulting dataset into PCA space, and then uses coarse-grained simulation on ASC to generate candidate folds. The central decision object is the Fold Admissibility Filter, which combines structural agreement, manifold proximity, physical stability, and pathway connectivity into one ranking score.

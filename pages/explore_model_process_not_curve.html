We model the process, not the curve
SPR DOI pending

Abstract

This presentation compresses the SPR argument to one line of force: both time scales, $t \to 0$ and $t \to \infty$, should be spanned by modeling alone. The initial SPR response and the final adsorption isotherm are therefore treated as two limits of one adsorption process rather than two disconnected data products. It functions as the compact companion to the longer SPR pitch.

Abstract

This presentation serves as the structural and conceptual front door to the Calyr.ai program. It frames inference as the intersection of instrument-derived constraints, introduces typed computational structure through Nexus Language, and positions SAXS-style structural ambiguity as a motivating case for model-governed reconstruction. Within the new Explore organization, it functions as the entry presentation for the SAXS track because it anchors the structural side of the platform.

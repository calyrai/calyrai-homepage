From signal to decision
SAXS DOI pending

Abstract

This presentation argues for the necessity of models in the strongest possible sense. Experimental signals are indirect, ambiguous, and underdetermined; abstraction alone makes them manageable, but only models make them meaningful. The talk connects SAXS, SPR, and model-based filtering through a Bayesian reading of interpretation: data removes what cannot be true, constraints rank what remains plausible, and decision emerges from a structured model space rather than from naive inversion.

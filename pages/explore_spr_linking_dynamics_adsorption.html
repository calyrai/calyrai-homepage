SPR · Presentation 01

From data to model: linking dynamics and adsorption
SPR DOI pending

Abstract

This talk states the main SPR thesis in its strongest form: the same model must span the entire signal, from the initial limit $t \to 0$ to the equilibrium limit $t \to \infty$. That means the early-time SPR trace and the long-time adsorption isotherm are not separate fitting targets but two consequences of one generative binding model. The presentation serves as the leading SPR record because it makes that double-sided modeling requirement explicit.

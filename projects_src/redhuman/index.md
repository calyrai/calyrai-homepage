<section id="overview" class="project-section">
  <h3>Overview</h3>
  <p>Redhuman frames metabolic reconstruction as a structured state-estimation problem where pathway annotation, thermodynamic feasibility and reduced network views remain coupled.</p>
</section>

<section id="methods" class="project-section">
  <h3>Methods</h3>
  <p>The project links curated stoichiometric models, reduced subnetworks and free-energy constraints so flux spaces stay interpretable rather than collapsing into opaque optimization artifacts.</p>
</section>

<section id="position" class="project-section">
  <h3>Constellation Position</h3>
  <p>Redhuman is the human-scale metabolic branch of the platform, extending the reduced-model logic from RedGEM toward larger curated biological systems.</p>
</section>

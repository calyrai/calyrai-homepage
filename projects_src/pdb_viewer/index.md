<section id="overview" class="project-section">
  <h3>Overview</h3>
  <p>The PDB Viewer complements the SAXS-facing tools by giving a direct structural landing page. Instead of exposing the raw viewer path first, the homepage now explains what the viewer is for and how it fits the broader platform.</p>
</section>

<section id="role" class="project-section">
  <h3>Role</h3>
  <p>This is the structure-side companion to scattering analysis: inspect models, compare structural hypotheses and keep geometry visible while the scattering and workflow layers evolve around it.</p>
</section>

<section id="position" class="project-section">
  <h3>Constellation Position</h3>
  <p>The PDB Viewer is the geometry-oriented browser tool inside the public Calyr ecosystem, paired with the SAXS-facing interfaces and analysis views.</p>
</section>

<section id="overview" class="project-section">
  <h3>Overview</h3>
  <p>RedGEM is the metabolic-reduction branch in the constellation: reduce large models into a legible core, preserve thermodynamic plausibility and keep the reduced system useful for interpretation.</p>
</section>

<section id="methods" class="project-section">
  <h3>Methods</h3>
  <p>The work combines topology-aware reduction, pathway selection and constraint-based reasoning so the reduced model keeps structural logic instead of collapsing into a black-box compression.</p>
</section>

<section id="position" class="project-section">
  <h3>Constellation Position</h3>
  <p>In the research constellation, RedGEM sits beside SAXS and PCA as another way of turning complex spaces into navigable lower-dimensional structure.</p>
</section>

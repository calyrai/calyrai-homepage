# Online Founding Austria

Consolidated working page for founding, trade registration, tax, and social insurance - fully digital

## 1. Objective

This page consolidates the key official online entry points for digital business founding in Austria. It is optimized for sole proprietors and can be extended later toward a GmbH setup.

## 2. Recommended Sequence

- Ensure ID Austria is active

- Activate USP / Mein USP access

- Submit online trade registration

- Set up FinanzOnline and tax registration

- Complete SVS insurance registration/check

- Set up bank account, invoicing logic, and compliance basics

## 3. Digital Identity

End-to-end online processing is easiest with ID Austria.

[ID Austria Information](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/id-austria.html)[ID Austria Portal](https://id-austria.gv.at/)

## 4. WKO and Founding

WKO is the best starting point for legal form and trade-law orientation.

[WKO: Start a Business](https://www.wko.at/oe/gruendung/start)[WKO: Trade Registration](https://www.wko.at/oe/gruendung/gewerbeanmeldung)[WKO: Legal Forms](https://www.wko.at/oe/gruendung/uebersicht-rechtsformen)

## 5. USP and Electronic Founding

The Unternehmensserviceportal is Austria's central digital gateway for business administration services.

[USP Founding World](https://startup.usp.gv.at/index.html)[USP: Founding](https://startup.usp.gv.at/gruendung)[USP: Electronic Founding](https://startup.usp.gv.at/gruendung/elektronische-gruendung)[Mein USP Login](https://mein.usp.gv.at/)[USP Registration](https://www.usp.gv.at/hilfe-und-support/registrieren.html)[USP: All Services](https://www.usp.gv.at/services/mein-usp/alle-services.html)

## 6. Direct Trade Registration

For a direct online trade filing, use the following USP service.

[USP: Online Trade Registration Form](https://www.usp.gv.at/services/mein-usp/GISxP.html)[GISA Portal](https://www.gisa.gv.at/)[GISA Search](https://www.gisa.gv.at/abfrage)

## 7. Tax Office and FinanzOnline

After registration, set up the tax side digitally and consistently.

[FinanzOnline Start](https://finanzonline.bmf.gv.at/)[FinanzOnline Login with ID Austria](https://finanzonline.bmf.gv.at/fon/oautheidlogin.do)[BMF: FinanzOnline Access](https://www.bmf.gv.at/public/informationen/fon-zugangsinformationen.html)[BMF Forms Service](https://service.bmf.gv.at/service/anwend/formulare/)

### Key Tax Topics

- Tax number and tax registration

- Income tax

- VAT and small-business regime

- Recurring declarations in FinanzOnline

### Relevant Form Anchor

**Verf24** is the key form reference for natural persons and is often relevant for sole proprietors.

## 8. SVS and Social Insurance

For self-employed founders, SVS is the key institution for insurance and contributions.

[SVS Start](https://www.svs.at/)[SVS: Online Insurance Registration](https://www.svs.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.899587&portal=svsportal)

## 9. Quick Start Checklist

| Step | Task | Online Entry Point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Activate or verify ID Austria | ID Austria / oesterreich.gv.at |
| 2 | Create USP account and verify login | Mein USP |
| 3 | Submit online trade registration | USP GISxP / GISA |
| 4 | Prepare tax registration | FinanzOnline / Verf24 |
| 5 | Finalize SVS insurance registration | SVS Online |
| 6 | Set up banking, invoicing, and document flow | internal |

## 10. Practical Notes

- For a simple sole-proprietor setup, USP, FinanzOnline, and SVS usually cover the full digital path.

- For regulated trades, additional evidence and documents are required.

- If you use the small-business VAT regime, keep your VAT logic consistent from day one.

- When scaling toward a GmbH, USP and FinanzOnline remain relevant, while notary and company register steps are added.

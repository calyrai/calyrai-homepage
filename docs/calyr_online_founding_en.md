# Online Founding Austria

Consolidated working page for founding, trade registration, tax, and social insurance - fully digital.

## 1. Objective

This page consolidates key official online entry points for digital business founding in Austria. It is optimized for sole proprietors and can be extended later toward a GmbH setup.

**Current status:** trade registration was completed via the GISA server. This is the central filing path for this workflow.

## 2. Recommended Sequence

- Ensure ID Austria is active.
- Confirm direct filing path through the GISA server.
- Submit online trade registration via the GISA server.
- Set up FinanzOnline and tax registration.
- Complete SVS insurance registration/check.
- Set up bank account, invoicing logic, and compliance basics.

## 3. Digital Identity

End-to-end online processing is easiest with ID Austria.

- [ID Austria Information](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/id-austria.html)
- [ID Austria Portal](https://id-austria.gv.at/)

## 4. WKO and Founding

WKO is the best starting point for legal form and trade-law orientation.

- [WKO: Start a Business](https://www.wko.at/oe/gruendung/start)
- [WKO: Trade Registration](https://www.wko.at/oe/gruendung/gewerbeanmeldung)
- [WKO: Legal Forms](https://www.wko.at/oe/gruendung/uebersicht-rechtsformen)

## 5. USP and Electronic Founding

The Unternehmensserviceportal is a supporting gateway for business administration services and preparation.

- [USP Founding World](https://startup.usp.gv.at/index.html)
- [USP: Founding](https://startup.usp.gv.at/gruendung)
- [USP: Electronic Founding](https://startup.usp.gv.at/gruendung/elektronische-gruendung)

## 6. Central Trade Registration Path (GISA)

The central route for trade filing in this workflow is the GISA server. Use USP only as the handoff when needed.

- [USP: Online Trade Registration Form](https://www.usp.gv.at/services/mein-usp/GISxP.html)
- [GISA Portal](https://www.gisa.gv.at/)
- [GISA Search](https://www.gisa.gv.at/abfrage)

## 7. Tax Office and FinanzOnline

After registration, set up the tax side digitally and consistently.

- [FinanzOnline Start](https://finanzonline.bmf.gv.at/)
- [FinanzOnline Login with ID Austria](https://finanzonline.bmf.gv.at/fon/oautheidlogin.do)
- [BMF: FinanzOnline Access](https://www.bmf.gv.at/public/informationen/fon-zugangsinformationen.html)
- [BMF Forms Service](https://service.bmf.gv.at/service/anwend/formulare/)

### Key Tax Topics

- Tax number and tax registration
- Income tax
- VAT and small-business regime
- Recurring declarations in FinanzOnline

### Relevant Form Anchor

**Verf24** is a key form reference for natural persons and is often relevant for sole proprietors.

## 8. SVS and Social Insurance

For self-employed founders, SVS is the key institution for insurance and contributions.

- [SVS Start](https://www.svs.at/)
- [SVS: Online Insurance Registration](https://www.svs.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.899587&portal=svsportal)

## 9. Quick Start Checklist

| Step | Task | Online Entry Point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Activate or verify ID Austria | ID Austria / oesterreich.gv.at |
| 2 | Confirm GISA filing access with ID Austria | GISA portal + ID Austria |
| 3 | Submit online trade registration (completed) | GISA server (USP handoff optional) |
| 4 | Prepare tax registration | FinanzOnline / Verf24 |
| 5 | Finalize SVS insurance registration | SVS Online |
| 6 | Set up banking, invoicing, and document flow | internal |

## 10. Practical Notes

- For this setup, GISA is the central trade-filing step, with FinanzOnline and SVS as the next core systems.
- ID Austria plus GISA should be treated as the baseline pair for the filing workflow.
- For regulated trades, additional evidence and documents are required.
- If you use the small-business VAT regime, keep your VAT logic consistent from day one.
- When scaling toward a GmbH, USP and FinanzOnline remain relevant, while notary and company register steps are added.

AORTA

AORTA is the CALYR-AFLOW extension for flow, thrombogenicity and inverse-flocking disassembly instrumentation under non-therapeutic boundaries.

Overview

AORTA follows the CALYR-AFLOW scientific loop: simulation generates hypotheses, tomography validates flow and thrombus-relevant states, feature extraction structures evidence, and feedback updates model constraints.

Execution Layer

ASC and OpenFOAM are used for high-throughput hemodynamic parameter exploration.

Validation Layer

Tomography provides spatial and temporal evidence to calibrate simulation outputs.

Multi-scale Layer

ALMMSP captures continuum-discrete coupling and thrombus-relevant dynamics.

Knowledge Layer

Nexus links results, features, constraints and publication-ready outputs.

Architecture

The proposal implements three connected loops: forward loop (specification to publication), reverse loop (publication evidence back to constraints), and validation loop (simulation-tomography-model update).

This turns linear CFD analysis into a recursive and reusable scientific system.

Interactive Proposal Layers

Switch layers to inspect the base CALYR-AFLOW logic and the AORTA inverse-flocking addon.

Core objectives: ASC-based CFD workflow, ALMMSP integration, tomography-grounded validation, and Nexus knowledge representation.

  • Objective 1: Robust ASC-based CFD execution.
  • Objective 2: Multi-scale thrombus-relevant dynamics via ALMMSP.
  • Objective 3: Tomographic validation of internal flow structures.
  • Objective 4: Structured evidence graph in Nexus.

Addon framing: thrombus network dissolution modeled as inverse flocking under mechanistic instrumentation constraints.

Clot or fibrin-gel state to holography to scattering profile to topology reconstruction to perturbation to dissolution pathway inference.
Ultrasound and microbubble directions, focused ultrasound, photothermal directions, and chemical controls remain in mechanistic preclinical scope.
Adds disassembly pathway inference, perturbation-response mapping, and risk-aware non-occlusive endpoint criteria to the thrombogenicity layer.

Workpackage orientation from the current proposal draft:

  • WP1: ASC and CFD pipeline foundation.
  • WP2: Multi-scale modeling and thrombus-relevant indicators.
  • WP3: Tomographic validation and calibration.
  • WP4: Evidence structuring, publication, and model update loops.

Safety Boundary

The inverse-flocking addon is explicitly non-therapeutic and non-clinical in the current phase. Scope is ex vivo and in vitro mechanistic instrumentation research only.

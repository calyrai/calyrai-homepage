Project

SAXS — Multi‑State Structural Analysis

Pair-distance distributions, free-energy modes, and browser-scale analysis — treating scattering as a mode sensor.

Back to Projects Explore Map

Overview

SAXS is a workbench for structural signals under mixtures: infer distributions and state compositions from 1D scattering while keeping the math legible and the tooling fast.

Methods

The focus is on decompositions into modes: compute stable projections, interpret them as perturbations of a free-energy landscape, and compare conditions as movements in a low-dimensional latent space.

Overview

The PDB Viewer complements the SAXS-facing tools by giving a direct structural landing page. Instead of exposing the raw viewer path first, the homepage now explains what the viewer is for and how it fits the broader platform.

Role

This is the structure-side companion to scattering analysis: inspect models, compare structural hypotheses and keep geometry visible while the scattering and workflow layers evolve around it.

Constellation Position

The PDB Viewer is the geometry-oriented browser tool inside the public Calyr ecosystem, paired with the SAXS-facing interfaces and analysis views.

# BRIX Surrogate Applications

Each second-level heading matches an application `id` in `content/books.yaml`. BRIX renders these narratives and their YAML-defined flows into the platform page.

## stampform

A programmable stamping process that couples contact, pressure, pull-off, and fixation to create repeatable material structures.

## oil

A material image system in which pigment recipes, relief height, transparency, and surface texture become manufacturing variables.

## chrom

An engineered chromatography body that replaces a random packed bed with designed flow paths, active surfaces, and ligand zones.

## aorta

A vessel surrogate connecting geometry, wall mechanics, flow, and local material gradients to experimental validation.

## nano

A multimodal nanoparticle-surface application that fuses Oracle-governed Cryo-EM morphology with SAXS ensemble constraints before modeling distribution, density, topography, coating, surface chemistry, and function.

## doe-bmca

An experimental-design surrogate that structures factors, responses, interactions, and BMCA-relevant methods in one design space.

## alphafold

A decision layer around structure predictions that exposes confidence, uncertain regions, provenance, and experimental follow-up through SAXS or other methods.

## saxs

An Oracle-originated and Oracle-registered SAXS workflow from I(q) and metadata through quality control, shape parameters, model comparison, residuals, validation, and reusable evidence.

## matomic

An independent modeling space for Matomic parameters, boundary conditions, simulation, sensitivity, prediction, and validation.

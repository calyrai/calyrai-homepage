# CALYR White Papers

Only public abstracts belong in this file. Registered and restricted editions are stored outside the public repository and are never compiled into public artifacts.

## wp-stampform
STAMPFORM describes a programmable transfer process in which material behavior, stamp geometry, contact, release, and fixation form one testable manufacturing loop.

## wp-oil-print
Oil Print treats pigment composition, transparency, relief, texture, and registration as variables of a directly manufactured material image.

## wp-chromstamp
ChromStamp investigates designed flow paths and functional surface zones as an alternative to relying exclusively on random packed beds in protein chromatography.

## wp-aorta
Aorta connects vessel geometry, boundary conditions, hemodynamics, wall mechanics, material gradients, surrogate prediction, and experimental validation.

## wp-nano
Nano Particle Surfaces combines Oracle-governed Cryo-EM morphology and SAXS ensemble constraints to reason about particle patterns and surface function.

## wp-doe-bmca
DOE · BMCA uses explicit factors, responses, constraints, uncertainty, and confirmation experiments to build a traceable surrogate-assisted design space.

## wp-alphafold
The AlphaFold concept joins Oracle provenance, QTY hypotheses, an explicit external-service boundary, confidence interpretation, SAXS-surrogate comparison, and experimental decisions.

## wp-saxs
The SAXS surrogate learns calibrated relationships among scattering evidence, simulations, QTY and AlphaFold hypotheses, structural descriptors, residuals, and experimental feedback.

## wp-matomic
Matomic describes a unit-aware material modelling loop with explicit boundary conditions, sensitivity, surrogate acceleration, applicability limits, and validation.

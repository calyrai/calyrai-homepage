Team

Scientific systems work led by Rupert Tscheliessnig with a cooperative, research-first collaboration model.

Primary role

Rupert Tscheliessnig is a computational biophysicist and AI systems architect focused on interpretable scientific software. The work combines structural biophysics, data-native modeling, and explainable workflows across SAXS, SPR, and coupled inference.

Base

Vienna

Domain

Structural biophysics, scientific ML, interpretable systems

Working style

Co-design with transparent assumptions and traceable decisions

Cooperative character

Collaboration protocol

  • Start from a shared scientific question, not from tooling.
  • Make model assumptions explicit and jointly reviewable.
  • Iterate in short loops: hypothesis, implementation, interpretation, revision.

Collaboration formats

  • Research exchange on SAXS and SPR interpretation pipelines.
  • Prototype development for model-based scientific workflows.
  • Technical advisory for interpretable AI in data-intensive labs.

Professional focus

  • Semantic workflow design for reproducible scientific analysis.
  • Integration of theory, data, and execution into one model layer.
  • Readable outputs for expert teams and external stakeholders.

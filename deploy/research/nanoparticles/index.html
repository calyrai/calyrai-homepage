02

OBJECTIVE

Model the S-layer as the outer molecular interface through which ions, molecules, and nanoparticles encounter and traverse a structured biological surface.

—

SbpA is the bacterial reference system. The modelling framework extends to archaeal S-layer interfaces and other nanostructured surfaces.

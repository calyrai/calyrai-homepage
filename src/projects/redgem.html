Overview

RedGEM is the metabolic-reduction branch in the constellation: reduce large models into a legible core, preserve thermodynamic plausibility and keep the reduced system useful for interpretation.

Methods

The work combines topology-aware reduction, pathway selection and constraint-based reasoning so the reduced model keeps structural logic instead of collapsing into a black-box compression.

Constellation Position

In the research constellation, RedGEM sits beside SAXS and PCA as another way of turning complex spaces into navigable lower-dimensional structure.
